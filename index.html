    ABOUT ME

    Hey, I'm KNGX DIOR

    KNGX DIOR is the artist and producer name of Mario, a 24-year-old producer driven by a deep love for pop and electronic music, with a particular obsession for the sound, energy and attitude of the 2000s.

    He started producing music in late 2018, initially as a passion that slowly evolved into a full-time creative pursuit. Over the years, KNGX DIOR has developed a signature sound built around catchy melodies, polished electronic production, club-driven rhythms and the unmistakable influence of 2000s pop.

    His production credits include collaborations with artists such as Princess Paparazzi on the single “Got This Feelin’” and Bleed Red on their latest album, alongside a growing catalog of collaborations with other artists. Tracks such as “Circles,” “Only Lover” and “Naughty” are among the releases leading into KNGX DIOR’s upcoming debut project — a body of work deeply rooted in the sounds and aesthetics that shaped his musical identity.

    Beyond his own releases, KNGX DIOR creates beats for artists looking for something more than a generic instrumental. Every production is built with a strong focus on songwriting, character and atmosphere — whether it’s a glossy pop record, an electro-pop anthem, a club-ready track or a darker Y2K-inspired production.

    For KNGX DIOR, the goal is simple: make records that feel familiar, but still sound like something new.
