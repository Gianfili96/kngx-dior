    FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

    Still have questions?

    Send me a message

    Can I use a beat for free?

    Yes, after a "free download" you can use the beat for non-profit purposes such as demos, practice, or free uploads to platforms like YouTube or SoundCloud—as long as you're not making money from it. However, you must still credit me properly (e.g., "Prod. by Manta Made It"). If you plan to distribute your song commercially, monetize it, or perform it publicly for profit, you'll need to purchase a proper license.

    Will the voice tags be removed once I purchase a license?

    Yes. All licenses come with the untagged version(s) of the beat(s). Regardless of which license you purchase.

    Will I own the beat once I purchase a license?

    No. All purchases made from the store are non-exclusive, meaning other artists can use the same beat as well. If you're interested in exclusive rights, please send me an email with the beat title and an offer.

    Can I download the files directly after I make the payment?

    Yes. Instantly after you've made the purchase, you will receive an email with links to download your files and license agreement(s).

    I want to buy a beat that you've already sold exclusively. What are my options?

    Unfortunately, once a beat is sold, I'm no longer allowed to license it to other artists. You can inquire for a custom beat , or look to find something else you like in our available catalog.

    I'm new to buying beats and need help choosing a license

    If you’re completely new to buying beats online, we recommend reading the information on our Licensing Page . If your questions aren't answered there, you can always send me a message.

    I'm looking for a beat but can't find it in your beat store?

    Go to the beat store on the homepage and use the search bar at the top of the player to search for the title. If the beat doesn’t show up or is marked as ‘SOLD’, it’s no longer available to purchase.

    What's your price for Exclusive Rights?

    I take offers starting at $1,000 in consideration. I don’t sell exclusive rights to every artist. Please inquire using the contact form above and include your full name, artist name, the beat title you’re inquiring for and a reasonable offer.

    Can I get a refund?

    Due to the nature of my products I have a no-refund policy. Refunds will only be issued for the following reasons: (1) The wrong beat was delivered or not delivered at all or (2) accidental double-orders. Please refer to our Refund Policy for more information.

    Which payment methods do you accept?

    I accept PayPal, major credit card payments and Apple Pay!

    Is it possible to upgrade my license later?

    Yes, if you want to upgrade your license, please send me an email for a special discounted price.

    What should I do when my license expires?

    Every non-exclusive license expires in 10 years after purchase. Contact me for renewal options if the beat hasn't been sold exclusively.

    What happens if I purchase a non-exclusive license and someone else buys the exclusive afterward?

    If you purchase a non-exclusive license, you can continue using the beat under the terms of your license - even if someone else purchases the exclusive rights afterward. Your license remains valid for 10 years after purchase and cannot be renewed once expired. After an exclusive license is sold, the beat is removed from the store and no new licenses (exclusive or non-exclusive) will be issued.

    Can I use Content ID on my song after purchasing a license?

    No. Content ID registration (such as YouTube Content ID or similar fingerprinting services) is not allowed with any license unless you've obtained exclusive rights and have written permission from the producer. This ensures that other artists with valid licenses aren’t affected by automated copyright claims.

    Do I have to credit the producer?

    Yes. Proper credit is required on all releases (e.g., “Prod. by Manta Made It”) in the description and credit section. It is not necessary to put the credit in the title of the song or video.

    Using the audio tag is not necessary after purchasing a license.

    Do you offer custom beats or production services?

    Yes, custom production is available upon request. Feel free to reach out via the contact page with your vision and budget to get started.

    Do you offer mixing and mastering services?

    Not by default - but possibly upon request. If you're interested in having your track mixed or mastered, feel free to reach out with details, and I’ll let you know if it's something I can take on.
